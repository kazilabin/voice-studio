How it works
  1. Tap Copy AI prompt and paste it into ChatGPT/Claude with your story idea.
  2. Copy the script it writes, paste it on the Script tab.
  3. Tap Make audio. Voices, effects, sound and music are made and mixed.
  4. Listen, fix single lines (↻ or 🎤), then download.
